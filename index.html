Why Crumple

Most task apps punish you. This one rewards you.

ADHD brains run on immediate reward. A quiet checkbox does nothing. Streaks just become one more thing to fail. Crumple makes "done" a moment you can feel.

🗒️→🪨

Crumple = done

Finish a task and physically crush the sticky note — a satisfying, tactile little win, every single time.

🚫🔥

No streaks, no guilt

Miss a day? Nothing breaks. There's no streak to protect and no shame engine. You just pick up where you are.

🤏

Deliberately tiny

Not another overwhelming productivity system. Sticky notes you crumple. That's the whole idea — on purpose.

Who's behind this

Hi, I'm Denis.

Clinical psychologist PhD …and I have ADHD

I'm a clinical psychologist with a PhD — and I live with ADHD myself. I kept reaching for task apps that were built for neurotypical brains and quietly made mine feel worse. So I started building the tools I actually wished existed.

Crumple is the first. It's the beginning of a small line of honest, ADHD-first products from ADHD Lab — designed with the science and the lived experience behind them.
